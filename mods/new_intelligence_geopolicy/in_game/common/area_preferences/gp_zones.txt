###################################
# New Intelligence: Geopolicy - zones of interest (conquest area preferences, prefix gp_)
#
# Format: area_preferences/conquest_preferences.txt (preference_type, modifier, area/region lists, consider_capital_region).
# A country gets its preferences from gp_zone_effects.txt (switch on tag) in three stages (DESIGN section 2):
#   core   (always)                       modifier 6   - the areas the country must own to exist as itself
#   sphere (home-region share >= 0.25)    modifier 3   - what it historically contested next
#   far    (home-region share >= 0.55)    modifier 1.5 - projection of power / colonial reach
# Vanilla keeps its own preferences (castile_conquer_spain, russia_conquer_region, ottoman_expansion_*...); ours add to them.
# GP TODO verify: the engine semantics of "modifier" (vanilla uses -0.9 .. 100; generic_conquer_home_region = 20). Our values
# stay inside the vanilla range and only ever order targets (core > sphere > far); no negative values are used so that
# nothing can stack below -1 with a vanilla negative preference on the same area.
# Area / region keys: in_game/map_data/definitions.txt (verified by tools/check_geopolicy_refs.sh against the map).
###################################
