## 2. Features end to end

### 2.1 Headquarters regiments
* `cw_hq`: category `army_auxiliary` (reserves, excluded from combined arms, auxiliary art for free), `is_special`, buildable, `levy = no`, `max_strength = 0.1` (= 100 men, stats are additive on the category), `combat_power = 0.25`, `country_potential = { always = yes }`, `limit = cw_hq_limit`. No modifiers of its own — DESIGN rule "an HQ gives no bonus" holds (checked in §4).
* `cw_hq_colonial`: same body; `country_potential` = Age of Discovery or later AND `cw_has_overseas_theatre` (owns an overseas location, or keeps an army of ≥ 3 regiments on overseas ground); `location_potential = { is_overseas_for_owner = yes }` (players recruit it overseas only); `limit = cw_colonial_hq_limit`.
* Prices are additive per-unit overrides on the auxiliary category: build 100 − 95 = 5 gold, reinforce 0.5, upkeep 0.1 / month, manpower 10 % of normal. Goods demand: `paper = 0.01` per 1 000 men (a 100-man staff consumes nothing).

### 2.2 Limits and their cache
* `cw_age_hq_slots`: 0 / 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 by age (Traditions → Revolutions); forced to 0 while the "isolated military tradition" is active (condition inlined, see §2.10).
* `cw_size_hq_slots = floor(num_locations / 75)` (100 before the tuning pass).
* `cw_hq_limit = 1 + imperial slot + min(age slots, size slots)`, capped at 5, never below 1; the imperial slot is +1 at ≥ 300 own locations in every age (the Yuan, the Horde, Delhi, Japan at the 1337 start) and is not zeroed by the isolated tradition. The breakdown rows use `desc = "KEY"` loc keys (`SUBUNIT_LIMIT_BASE`, `CW_HQ_LIMIT_IMPERIAL`, `CW_HQ_LIMIT_AGE_AND_SIZE`, `CW_HQ_LIMIT_CAP`, `CW_HQ_LIMIT_MIN`).
* `cw_colonial_hq_limit`: 0 before the Age of Discovery or while isolated; else for each of the five continents that is not the capital's: +1 if the theatre is active (≥ 5 owned locations there that are `is_overseas_for_owner`, OR an own army of ≥ 3 regiments standing there on ground that `would_be_overseas_for` the owner), +1 if ≥ 25 locations there — own overseas land, or land of any subject at any depth (`any_subject_or_below`, because the AI holds its colonies through colonial nations) — and Age of Reformation+, +1 if ≥ 60 and Age of Absolutism+; global cap 4. America is one continent.
* The colonial limit walks the owned-location list up to ten times, so script evaluates it in exactly one place: `cw_cache_limits` writes `cw_hq_limit_v`, `cw_hq_count_v`, `cw_colonial_hq_limit_v`, `cw_colonial_hq_count_v` (country variables). Everything else compares the live engine counter `total_sub_unit_type_count:cw_hq_colonial` with `var:cw_colonial_hq_limit_v` (`cw_has_free_colonial_hq_slot`, `cw_colonial_hqs_above_limit`, `cw_colonial_hq_free_slots`, `cw_colonial_hq_excess`). The home limit is cheap and read live (`cw_has_free_hq_slot`, `cw_hq_free_slots`). `cw_cache_limits` runs at game start, every monthly pulse, every 15-day war tick, war start / end, new age, after every player button click and when automation is switched on. The engine reads `limit = cw_colonial_hq_limit` live for recruitment only.

### 2.3 Organization: the three tiers, the expeditionary bonus and the rebel malus
* Exempt units (`cw_unit_is_exempt`): navies, `is_exiled`, `has_only_prisoners`. Hired mercenary stacks are **not** exempt (deliberate, SPEC §16). Exempt armies get every mod modifier removed (`cw_clear_unit_modifiers`).
* Organized (`cw_unit_is_organized`) = `num_subunits >= 3` (`cw_min_subunits_organized`: the staff plus two other regiments) AND the right headquarters for the ground: a home HQ (`cw_hq`) anywhere, or a colonial HQ (`cw_hq_colonial`) on overseas ground (`cw_unit_is_overseas` = `unit_location = { would_be_overseas_for = prev.owner }`). A lone HQ or "HQ + one regiment" is never organized (tuning pass, research 15 §7 P2).
* Siege-only tier (`cw_unit_has_colonial_hq_at_home`): big enough, no home HQ, a colonial HQ on NON-overseas ground → `cw_no_hq_siege_only` (siege −90 %, assault −50 %, full combat strength; never varies with the game rule). A coloniser cannot ship colonial HQs home as extra siege armies (research 15 §2.4, DESIGN 2.4).
* `cw_update_unit_organization` (one army) picks one of the states through the setters `cw_set_unit_organized_home` (no modifier) / `cw_set_unit_organized_expeditionary` (`cw_expeditionary`, only with a colonial HQ on overseas ground) / `cw_set_unit_siege_only` / `cw_set_unit_unorganized`; every setter removes the modifiers of the other states. Unorganized → the penalty variant chosen by game rule `cw_rule_hq_penalty` (`cw_no_hq` standard: siege −90 %, assault −50 %, discipline −20 %, morale −25 %; mild −10/−10; harsh −30/−30 — the siege / assault terms are shared by all three; 1 year, mode replace, so the modifier dies by itself if the mod stops refreshing it) and the human owner's flag `cw_has_army_without_hq` for the hint. `cw_unit_has_hq_penalty` covers the three penalty variants only — the siege-only tier and `cw_rebel_disorder` are deliberately not "no-HQ penalties" (battle re-check, hint flag, GUI marker).
* `cw_update_army_organization` (country): clears the hint flag, then `every_army` → exempt ? clear : update. `cw_refresh_army_without_hq_flag` drops the flag after a player click when no unorganized (`cw_unit_is_unorganized` = neither organized nor siege-only) non-exempt army is left.
* Rebels (`cw_rebel_disorder_country`, guarded by `is_rebel_country`): every army of a rebel country carries `cw_rebel_disorder` (discipline −20 %, morale −25 %, no siege term; 1 year, replace) — refreshed monthly and at `on_revolt_start` / `on_civil_war_start`; nothing else is ever done for rebels (no limits, no HQs). Pirates and mercenary-company countries stay untouched.
### 2.4 Scheduling
Vanilla hooks are only appended to (`on_actions = { cw_x }` lists merge across files): `monthly_country_pulse`, `on_game_started_after_lobby`, `on_new_age`, `on_war_declared`, `on_join_war`, `on_ending_war`, `on_battle_won`, `on_command_gained`, `on_revolt_start`, `on_civil_war_start` (hq_core); `monthly_country_pulse`, `on_war_declared`, `on_join_war`, `on_ending_war` (ai_stance); `on_siege_won`, `on_location_occupied` (warscore_rules).
* **Game start** (`cw_on_game_start`, global): `every_country = { limit = { cw_is_real_country = yes } cw_game_start_country = yes }` → `cw_initialized`, first HQ into the largest army (`cw_initial_hq_grant`), organization pass, cache, seed `cw_hq_limit_seen`.
* **Monthly** (`cw_monthly_pulse`, trigger `cw_is_real_country`): cache → seed countries that never saw game start (released / formed / old save) → at war: `cw_run_hq_pass` + `cw_start_war_ticker`; at peace: the full pass only when `cw_peace_cooldown` is absent, then `set_variable = { name = cw_peace_cooldown months = 3 }` (staggered quarterly) → players: `cw_player_notifications`.
* **Rebels** (`cw_monthly_rebel_pulse`, trigger `is_rebel_country`, on the same monthly pulse; `cw_on_revolt_start` on `scope:target` of `on_revolt_start` / `on_civil_war_start`): `cw_rebel_disorder_country` refreshes `cw_rebel_disorder` on every rebel army — the only work the mod does for rebels.
* `cw_run_hq_pass` = `cw_update_army_organization` → AI: `cw_ai_relocate_hqs` → AI or `cw_auto_hq` players: `cw_ai_assign_hqs` → AI: `cw_cleanup_excess_hqs`.
* **War ticker** `cw_tick.1` (hidden country event): trigger `cw_is_real_country`, `at_war`, `NOT has_variable = cw_tick_scheduled`; immediate: cache, full pass, `trigger_event_silently = { id = cw_tick.1 days = 15 }`, then `set_variable = { name = cw_tick_scheduled days = 14 }`. The 14-day guard kills any duplicate chain; the monthly pulse re-arms a dead chain within a month.
* **War start** (`cw_on_war_start` from `on_war_declared` on `scope:actor` / `scope:recipient` / root, and from `on_join_war` on root): `cw_war_start_country` — idempotent through the 2-day variable `cw_war_start_done`; drops the peace cooldown, caches, runs the pass, starts the ticker.
* **War end** (`cw_on_war_end`, once per side): drop the cooldown so the first peaceful pulse does a full pass; cache.
* **New age** (`cw_on_new_age`): cache, let AI / automation fill the new slot, notify the player.
* **Battle** (`cw_on_battle` from `on_battle_won`; `scope:actor` = winner unit, `scope:target` = loser unit, `?=` skips a wiped unit): `cw_battle_recheck_unit` on both — if a human-owned army has no HQ and no penalty (it was organized at the last pass) fire `cw_events.2` (once per 30 days via `cw_hq_lost_notice`), then re-check the unit. `on_battle_lost` carries the same pair and is not hooked.
* **Commander assigned** (`cw_on_command`, `scope:unit`): `cw_recheck_unit` (catches player merges / splits).

### 2.5 Script placement (AI, or players with automation)
* `cw_ai_assign_hqs`: if the country has no home HQ at all → `cw_initial_hq_grant` (largest idle land army, any size); else if a home slot is free → one `ordered_army` over `cw_eligible_for_home_hq` (army, not in combat / retreat, ≥ `cw_min_subunits_for_ai_hq` = 3 regiments, on land, not exempt, no HOME HQ, not on overseas ground — an army whose only HQ is a colonial one brought home is a candidate), ranked `num_subunits` + 1000 if it has regulars / + 500 if levies, `max = cw_hq_free_slots`, `add_subunit = unit_type:cw_hq` + `cw_home_hq_added` (sets the state at once; an army still below 3 regiments keeps the penalty). Colonial: from the Age of Discovery, with a free cached slot, `local_var:cw_col_free = cw_colonial_hq_free_slots`, then `cw_ai_assign_colonial_hq_on = { continent = X }` for the five continents: a theatre may hold 1 HQ army, 2 with ≥ 25 locations there from the Age of Reformation, 3 with ≥ 60 from Absolutism — own overseas land or land of any subject at any depth, the same tiers that grant the slots; the largest `cw_eligible_for_colonial_hq` army (no HQ of any type, overseas ground) on that continent gets `cw_hq_colonial` + `cw_colonial_hq_added` (expeditionary bonus overseas, siege-only tier otherwise), and the counter is decremented through the `owner` link.
* `cw_ai_relocate_hqs` (AI only), before the placement of the same pass: (1) while a "stranded" home HQ exists (stack below 3 regiments, idle on land, `cw_unit_has_stranded_home_hq`) AND an eligible HQ-less home army waits, every stranded home HQ is stripped (`cw_strip_home_hq_from_unit`); (2) every "misplaced" colonial HQ — on non-overseas land, where it only yields the siege-only tier — is stripped whatever the army's size (`cw_unit_has_misplaced_colonial_hq`), never at sea; (3) per theatre, rule 1 for stranded colonial HQs while an eligible HQ-less army waits on the same continent (`cw_ai_relocate_colonial_hq_on`).
* `cw_cleanup_excess_hqs` (AI only): count > limit → strip one HQ from each of the smallest HQ armies (idle, on land, not exempt), `max = cw_hq_excess`; the colonial half reads the cached limit and waits while any colonial-HQ army is at sea (a crossing must not cost the theatre's HQ).
* `cw_strip_home_hq_from_unit` / `cw_strip_colonial_hq_from_unit` remove ONE regiment per call (`cw_destroy_one_sub_unit_of_type`: `ordered_sub_unit` + `max = 1` + `destroy_subunit`) and decide the resulting state from the regiment list BEFORE the strip: the HQ is the only regiment → `destroy_unit`; more than 3 regiments before the strip (≥ 3 after it) with a colonial HQ on overseas ground → expeditionary, with another home HQ → organized, with a colonial HQ at home → siege-only; otherwise the penalty and the hint flag at once. Players are never force-disbanded (relocation and cleanup are AI-only).

### 2.7 War stances (module ai_stance)
* Ids: 0 unknown, 1 offensive, 2 defensive, 3 waiting, 4 harassing (`cw_stance_id_*`). Hooks: monthly, `on_war_declared` (actor + recipient), `on_join_war` (root), `on_ending_war` (clear only, when the country is at peace now). Entry point `cw_stance_update` (eligible = `cw_is_real_country`): at peace → `cw_clear_stance`; AI → `cw_update_war_stance`; human → `cw_update_player_enemy_stance`.
* `cw_find_main_enemy`: saves `scope:cw_self`; over `every_current_war` (civil wars skipped) takes the other side's leader (`defender_leader` if `is_an_attacker = scope:cw_self`, else `attacker_leader`) and keeps the one with the largest `military_strength` (scratch variable `cw_find_enemy_best`) as `scope:cw_enemy` (may not exist).
* Decision, in priority order (all relative to the main enemy; `relative_military_strength` = ours / (ours + theirs)): **harassing** if losing badly (`war_exhaustion >= 8`, or `war_score_of_country <= -25` in a war against the main enemy) or the ratio is ≤ 0.33 (≤ 0.40 for a cautious temperament; kept until it exceeds the bound + 0.05, i.e. 0.38), or its fielded `army_size` is ≤ 0.5 × the enemy's; **offensive** if the ratio is ≥ 0.57 (0.50 bold, 0.60 cautious; kept down to the bound − 0.04, i.e. 0.53) and its war score against the main enemy is ≥ −10; **waiting** if this country is the ATTACKER against the main enemy AND (that war is younger than 1 year OR `army_size_percentage` < 0.8); else **defensive**. Temperament: bold ruler traits (`conqueror`, `expansionist`, `greedy`, `bold_fighter`, `zealot`, `malevolent`, `drunkard`, `naive`) or an `offensive_planner` leading the largest army; cautious (`craven`, `careful`, `calm`, `kind_hearted`, `benevolent`, `obsessive_perfectionist`) or a `defensive_planner`; both at once cancel out for the offensive bar.
* `cw_try_switch_stance`: switch only when the stance differs and no `cw_stance_lock` exists; a switch applies `cw_stance_<x>` (country modifier, 2 months, replace), stores `cw_stance`, sets `cw_stance_lock` for 2 months and `cw_stance_changed` for 1 month; otherwise the current modifier is refreshed. Modifier levers are AI-only valuation types (`aggressiveness_modifier`, `carefulness_modifier`, `defence_importance_modifier`, `expected_warscore_modifier`, `manpower_importance_modifier`, `win_war_chance_threshold`, `ai_months_between_wars`) plus `regiment_reinforcement_speed = 0.1` on waiting — the only gameplay term.
* Player mirror: `cw_enemy_stance_source` = main enemy; `cw_enemy_stance` = the enemy's `var:cw_stance` if readable (AI, has a stance, no `cw_stance_changed` — the one-month intelligence delay, separate from the 2-month lock) else 0. With game rule `cw_stance_events_on`, a change of a readable stance for the SAME main enemy fires `cw_stance.1` (text per stance via `first_valid`).
* Subject stance `cw_escort_military_stance` (support armies / sieges 10, merge 10, hunt 4, chase into enemy land 4, defend own territory 6, carpet siege 1, naval transport 3, `confront_factor = 0.5`): assigned by the player in the subjects screen only (the AI overlord's choice is C++). Its icon is not shipped (the mod has no `gfx/` folder) — open item in the file header.
